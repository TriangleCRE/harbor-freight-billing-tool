Method: the full water/sewer bill is split across eligible tenants by each one's share of submeter usage (gallons — no HCF conversion), so the whole city bill is recovered. The Fire Monitoring (FM) charge — technically a second City of Harrisonburg water account for the fire-suppression line — is split by each tenant's fixed lease pro-rata share instead of usage. Usage for each bill is pulled from the readings log; when the logged reads don't line up with the bill period exactly, the closest available reads are used and shown. Bills with a missing city invoice are marked estimated. Data auto-saves to the shared database and can be exported to a JSON file at any time.
